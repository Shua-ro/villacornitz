The Experience

Villa Cornitz Resort is a public swim facility situated in a rural barangay setting, surrounded by open fields. The property provides a clean, accessible environment for local groups and families looking to gather and swim.

The central feature of the resort is the swimming pool, which is supplied continuously by a fresh, natural underground water source. This source ensures the water remains consistently crystal clear and naturally low in temperature, providing an efficient way to counter the local climate at an affordable price point.

  • NATURAL UNDERGROUND SOURCE
  • ICE-COLD REFRESHING WATER
  • FREE COTTAGE USE INCLUDED
  • AFFORDABLE LOCAL ENTRANCE
